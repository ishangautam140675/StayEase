📅 WEEK 1: Team Formation, Guide Selection & Abstract
Week 1

Goal: Form 4-member team, obtain faculty guide approval, and formulate the project abstract.

Problem Statement: Students and young professionals relocating to new cities suffer from 15%–30% broker commissions and manual hostel registers causing frequent double-bookings. StayEase was conceptualized to provide a zero-brokerage reservation portal with free worldwide geospatial intelligence for guests, paired with an administrative property management dashboard for owners.

📋 WEEK 2: Software Requirements Specification (SRS)
Week 2

Goal: Finalize 6 Functional Requirements (FRs) and 4 Non-Functional Requirements (NFRs).

  • Functional: FR-1 (Auth & RBAC), FR-2 (Free OpenStreetMap Geocoding & 100km Radar), FR-3 (Zero-Overbooking Transactional Booking), FR-4 (Digital Vouchers & Web Check-In), FR-5 (Room Inventory & Maintenance Blackouts), FR-6 (ADR/RevPAR Financial Analytics & Audit Logs).
  • Non-Functional: Strict ACID concurrency via PESSIMISTIC_WRITE row locking, BCrypt salted password hashing, sub-500ms catalog queries, and 100% open-source map integration with zero API billing risks.

4. Weeks 5–8: Module Ownership (4 Students = 4 Modules)

MODULE 1 (STUDENT 1): Worldwide Geospatial Discovery & Open Map Engine

Engineered Leaflet.js interactive pins, Komoot Photon 220ms debounced typeahead autocomplete, Haversine spherical formula in Java (calculateDistanceKm), and dual-tier Overpass/Nominatim bounding box scanning up to 100 km with zero API cost.

MODULE 2 (STUDENT 2): Customer Booking Engine & Concurrency Control

Implemented database PESSIMISTIC_WRITE row locking (SELECT ... FOR UPDATE) on rooms, canonical date overlap algorithm (existing.checkIn < new.checkOut) AND (existing.checkOut > new.checkIn), same-day back-to-back stays, and digital printable voucher receipts.

MODULE 3 (STUDENT 3): Property Operations, Revenue Analytics & Compliance Audit

Built the Room Maintenance Blackout scheduler (repairs blocking room availability), Seasonal Price rules, ADR (Average Daily Rate) and RevPAR (Revenue Per Available Room) computations, and immutable compliance CSV audit trail export.

MODULE 4 (STUDENT 4): Authentication, Spring Security, Notifications & Cron Reminders

Engineered Spring Security 6 RBAC (ROLE_CUSTOMER vs ROLE_ADMIN), BCrypt salted password hashing, CSRF synchronizer tokens, ThreadPoolTaskExecutor async emails, and @Scheduled daily check-in reminder alerts.
